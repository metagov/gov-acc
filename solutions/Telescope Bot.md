An automated tool that flags high-signal Discord comments for ethnographic review and archival. Telescope Bot captures the informal governance insights that would otherwise be lost in the relentless scroll of chat platforms, recognizing that some of the most important governance intelligence lives in ephemeral conversations.

## How It Works

Telescope Bot monitors Discord channels for governance-relevant comments and flags them for human review. Rather than trying to archive everything (which creates information overload) or relying on participants to manually save important messages (which rarely happens), the bot uses heuristics and pattern matching to identify high-signal comments that deserve preservation.

The flagging criteria can include message engagement metrics (reactions, replies, thread depth), keyword patterns, mentions of governance-related topics, or machine learning classifiers trained on previously flagged messages. Flagged messages are surfaced to designated reviewers — typically governance researchers or facilitators — who decide whether to archive them in a more permanent knowledge base.

The ethnographic dimension is important. Telescope Bot isn't just an archival tool; it's designed to support qualitative governance research. By systematically surfacing informal discourse, it enables researchers and practitioners to understand the social dynamics that underlie formal governance processes. Informal conversations in Discord often reveal the real reasons behind governance decisions — disagreements, concerns, and insights that never make it into formal proposal discussions.

This approach acknowledges a fundamental truth about decentralized governance: much of the actual deliberation happens in chat, not in formal governance forums. Ignoring that reality means missing most of the governance intelligence.

## Problems Addressed

- [[Institutional Amnesia]] — Captures and preserves governance-relevant insights from ephemeral chat platforms that would otherwise be lost within days, building a persistent record of informal governance discourse
- [[Informal Power]] — Makes visible the informal conversations where governance decisions are actually shaped, enabling analysis of who influences governance outside of formal channels

## Maturity Assessment

At Custom-to-Product (ex 3), Telescope Bot represents a relatively simple but effective approach to a real problem. The core technology — message monitoring, flagging, and archival — is straightforward compared to more ambitious knowledge infrastructure projects. Its maturity advantage comes from this simplicity: it works with existing platforms and workflows rather than requiring behavioral change. The primary limitation is that it depends on human reviewers to evaluate flagged content, which doesn't scale easily across large organizations with multiple active Discord servers.
