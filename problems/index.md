Preliminary findings from 50 Harmonica structured interviews. Eleven governance problems identified so far, ranked by composite urgency score. Problem descriptions were synthesized using AI-assisted analysis and may contain elaborations beyond what participants explicitly stated — see [[Phase 1 Results#Methodology|Methodology]] for details. The urgency formula weights breadth (how many interviewees raised it) at 60% and depth (average messages discussing it) at 40%. Phase 1 research is ongoing — these rankings may evolve as additional data sources are integrated.

> [!info] How to read urgency scores
> **Breadth** measures how widespread the problem is across interviews. **Depth** measures how extensively participants discussed it. A problem with high breadth but low depth (like [[Voting Fatigue]]) is widely recognized but well-understood. A problem with lower breadth but high depth (like [[Over-Reliance on Game Theory]]) is a specialist concern that deeply engages those who encounter it.
