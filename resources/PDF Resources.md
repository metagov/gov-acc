disputes originate. Voting power is adjusted to prioritize diverse perspectives over concentrated influence. When a decision is challenged, the appeal process triggers a multi-tiered review, with each successive tier requiring a jury composed of informationally distinct members. This structure reduces the risk of coordinated manipulation or factional bias.
